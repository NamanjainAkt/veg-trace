The 5-Tier Verdict Protocol

v2.4 Spec

Every detected E-number, chemical synonym, and carrier additive is cross-referenced across five definitive verdict gates.

check
Vegetarian Tier 1

Exclusively mineral, botanical, or synthesized origins. Zero animal derivatives or animal processing aids (e.g. bone char).

close
Non-Vegetarian Tier 2

Contains slaughterhouse by-products, cochineal/carmine (E120), animal rennet, gelatin, or slaughter-derived tallowates.

alt_route
Source Dependent Tier 3

Dual-origin lipids & emulsifiers (e.g., E471 mono- and diglycerides, stearic acid). Requires batch CoA verification.

visibility_off
Source Undisclosed Tier 4

Generic label declarations (such as generic “Natural Flavors” or “Enzymes”) lacking public manufacturer substantiation.

help_outline
Unknown Tier 5

Emerging novel food compounds or proprietary branded blends not yet cataloged in our clinical registry.
